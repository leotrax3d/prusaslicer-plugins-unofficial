# Gridfinity Bin Generator

**Plugin:** `dev.leotrax3d.box-generator` · **Menu:** Generators → Gridfinity Bin

Generates Gridfinity-compatible storage bins from the published 42 mm grid and 7 mm
height unit. Gridfinity was created by Zack Freedman; this independently implemented
generator is not affiliated with or endorsed by him or the Gridfinity community.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grid Units X / Y | 1 × 1 | Footprint in 42 mm grid cells |
| Height Units | 3 | Body height in 7 mm units |
| UltraLight Hollow Base | on | Hollows the foot to a 1.0 mm shell over a 1.0 mm floor. Applies to bins with one compartment and no magnets; multi-cell bins need at least 2 height units |
| Floor Thickness | 1.2 mm | Material above the locating foot, when the base is solid |
| Compartments Across / Deep | 1 × 1 | Separate internal pockets |
| Divider Thickness | 1.2 mm | Material left between pockets |
| 6×2 mm Magnet Pockets | off | Four 6.5×2.4 mm pockets per grid cell |
| Stacking Lip | on | Adds the upper locating rim |
| Front Label | empty | Engraves text into the front wall |

One unit is 41.5 × 41.5 mm outside, leaving 0.5 mm total clearance in a 42 mm grid; the
wall is a fixed 1.2 mm. The locating base is 4.75 mm high: 0.8 mm lower 45° ramp, 1.8 mm
vertical section and 2.15 mm upper 45° ramp. A bin of n height units is 7 n mm to the top
of its body, plus the stacking lip.

The shape follows a widely printed UltraLight bin, measured and rebuilt from the
published dimensions, not copied:

- **Stacking lip:** 0.7 mm 45° chamfer from 2.6 mm in, 1.8 mm vertical at 1.9 mm in, then
  45° out to a flat top 0.7 mm wide. The vertical face leaves the 37.2 mm foot of the
  bin above 0.25 mm per side. The knife edge of the full 4.4 mm profile is cut off,
  which prints cleanly and does not change the fit, so the lip is 3.7 mm tall.
- **Lip support:** with one compartment, the wall widens under the lip at 45° from 1.2 to
  2.6 mm, so the lip's inner edge does not overhang the cavity. With several
  compartments the dividers reach the top and the support is left out.
- **UltraLight base:** each foot is hollowed 1.0 mm inside its outer face, over a 1.0 mm
  floor, and opens into the cavity where its slope meets the wall. Between grid cells the
  body keeps a floor up to 5.75 mm.

All sloped parts are real meshes with exact 45° faces, generated by
`tools/gen-gridfinity-meshes.py`; CI fails if the committed STLs do not match the script.

- `gridfinity_foot.stl`, one per grid cell. Its corner radii shrink with the offset, from
  3.75 mm at the top to 0.8 mm at the bottom.
- `gridfinity_pocket.stl`, the hollow base, one per grid cell, as a negative volume.
- `gridfinity_lip_*.stl` and `gridfinity_support_*.stl`: the plugin API cannot stretch a
  loaded mesh, so lip and support are tiled. Each side of n units is 42 n − 8 mm between
  its corners: n − 1 pieces of 42 mm and one of 34 mm, rotated into place around the bin.
  The support pieces are negative volumes that remove the triangle below the slope.

## Implementation and validation

Each compartment is cut as an independent negative pocket. The solid material between
pockets therefore remains a divider even though PrusaSlicer sorts all positive volumes
before all negative volumes. This replaces the invalid ordering assumption in version
1.0.0.

The automated geometry test checks the 41.5 mm footprint, 7 mm height system, locating
foot, divider survival and four magnet pockets per occupied grid cell. A physical
Gridfinity baseplate and stacking test is still required before calling the fit proven.

included here; the dimensions are implemented directly.
